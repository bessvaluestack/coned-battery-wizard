# Glossary

**Alternative 3** — The Value Stack's capacity option for storage: a monthly $/kW credit on the net injection during the previous year's New York Control Area peak hour. See [Value Stack](value-stack.md).

**As-used demand** — On standby rates, each weekday's peak in set windows (8 AM to 6 PM and 8 AM to 10 PM in summer, 8 AM to 10 PM otherwise), billed per day in $/kW-day.

**Auto-DLM** — Rider AC's automated Dynamic Load Management program: events at 10 minutes' notice, 8 AM to midnight. Stands alone.

**Billing demand** — The highest average import over a 15-minute (or 30-minute) interval in a charge's window: the determinant demand charges bill on.

**CESIR** — Coordinated Electric System Interconnection Review: Con Edison's interconnection study under the NY SIR.

**Contract demand** — On standby rates, the most the account takes from the grid, billed every month in $/kW.

**Contracted Hours** — A network's CSRP event window, which also sets the Value Stack DRV hours.

**CSRP** — Commercial System Relief Program (Rider T): planned peak events, a reservation payment and a performance payment.

**Designated Technology** — Under General Rule 20.3.2(b), storage up to 1 MW is billed on standard rates rather than standby.

**DLRP** — Distribution Load Relief Program (Rider T): contingency events, a reservation payment and a performance payment.

**DRV** — Demand Reduction Value: the Value Stack's $/kWh credit on net injection in the network's call window, fixed for 10 years.

**Equivalent full cycle** — Discharge equal to the battery's nameplate energy. The annual cycle limit counts these.

**ESCO** — An energy services company: a third-party supplier of the energy, as opposed to Con Edison full service.

**HT / LT** — High tension (primary) and low tension (secondary) service.

**LBMP** — Location-based marginal price: NYISO's wholesale energy price, by zone and hour. The wizard uses the day-ahead price.

**LSRV** — Locational System Relief Value: a Value Stack $/kW-year credit where the location has MW left, paid on the lowest net injection in each event.

**MAC** — Monthly Adjustment Clause, a per-kWh delivery adjustment.

**MFC** — Merchant function charge, part of Con Edison's supply charges.

**MSC** — Market Supply Charge: Con Edison's full-service energy price, with a separate capacity charge.

**Net injection** — In an hour, what the account sends to the grid beyond what it takes. Value Stack credits are paid on it.

**NYISO zones** — J is New York City; H (Millwood) and I (Dunwoodie) cover Westchester.

**RDM** — Revenue decoupling mechanism, a per-kWh delivery adjustment by service class.

**Rider AC** — Con Edison's Dynamic Load Management programs: Term-DLM and Auto-DLM.

**Rider M** — Day-ahead hourly pricing for Con Edison full-service supply, with a capacity tag. Mandatory for some accounts.

**Rider R** — Con Edison's rider for distributed generation compensation, including the Value Stack (A.9(d) for storage).

**Rider T** — Con Edison's commercial demand response programs: CSRP and DLRP.

**Round-trip efficiency** — The share of charged energy that comes back out, AC to AC.

**SBC** — System Benefits Charge: a per-kWh surcharge (NYSERDA) and a per-kW demand surcharge.

**SC 2, SC 8, SC 9, SC 11** — Con Edison service classes: General - Small; Multiple Dwellings, Redistribution; General - Large; and buy-back service for exports.

**SDR** — Delivery revenue surcharge, a per-kWh (and, on standby, per-kW) adjustment.

**SIR** — The New York State Standardized Interconnection Requirements.

**Standby service** — General Rule 20 service for customers with on-site generation (Rates IV and V): a contract demand plus daily as-used demand, with no per-kWh delivery charge.

**Term-DLM** — Rider AC's Term Dynamic Load Management program: a multi-year, pay-as-bid contract with day-ahead calls.

**Time-of-day (TOD)** — Rates II and III: demand charges in weekday windows (8 AM to 6 PM and 8 AM to 10 PM), plus an all-hours charge.

**Usable depth of discharge** — The share of the nameplate energy the controller may use.

**Value Stack** — New York's compensation for distributed resources' exports: energy, capacity, DRV and LSRV components.
